Privacy policy

Last updated 30.09.2026

KeyFolio is built to keep your records on your own device. We don't run a server for the app, we don't have user accounts, and we don't collect any data from you.

What the app stores

The stays, rooms, notes, photos, videos, voice notes and meter readings you add are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. We can't see them, and nobody else can unless you share them.

Camera, microphone and photos

KeyFolio uses the camera and microphone only while you take a photo, record a video or record a voice note. It reads from your photo library only the items you pick to import, and saves to it only when you ask it to.

Location

If you allow it, KeyFolio adds your approximate location to a photo, video or voice note at the moment you capture it. It never tracks your location in the background. You can turn location tagging off in Settings, or deny the permission, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Sharing

When you share a report, videos or a backup, KeyFolio hands the files to your device's share sheet. Where they go next (email, a messaging app, a cloud drive) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies. KeyFolio doesn't send anything on its own.

Analytics, ads and tracking

KeyFolio has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Backups and deleting your data

Backups are files you create and keep yourself. To delete your data, delete a stay inside the app, or uninstall KeyFolio to remove everything it stored on the device. Copies you already shared or backed up aren't affected.

Children

KeyFolio is meant for students and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
