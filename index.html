KeyFolio

Move in & Move out records for students

KeyFolio helps you record the condition of your room when you move in, and compare it when you move out. Take photos and videos, write down what's already damaged, note meter readings and keys, then share a PDF report with your landlord or dorm office.

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account and no cloud.
