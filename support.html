Support

Found a bug, or have a question or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you. Please don't include personal photos or addresses in the issue, since issues are public.

Common questions

Where are my records stored?

Only on your device, inside KeyFolio. There's no account and nothing is uploaded.

How do I send a report to my landlord?

Open the stay, go to Reports and tap Share report. KeyFolio creates a PDF and opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send it by email, WhatsApp or any other app. Use Share report + videos to add videos as separate files.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

Go to Settings and create a backup, then save or send the backup file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install KeyFolio and restore the backup from Settings.

Why is the location missing on some photos?

Location is added only if you allowed it and your phone could get a fix at that moment. Indoors this can take a while or fail. KeyFolio shows the location as missing rather than guessing.

Can I change a record after saving it?

Yes. Edits are kept as new revisions, and the revision history shows what changed. Original photos and videos are never modified.

Is the report a legal document?

No. It's your own dated record of what you saw. It can help in a conversation or a dispute about your deposit, but KeyFolio doesn't decide who is responsible for damage.
